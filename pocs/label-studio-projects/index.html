    Notes

    Man/Woman/Child ≡ Male/Female/Child — every MFC project in this workspace uses the labels Man / Woman / Child; none currently use the literal words "Male"/"Female", but they mean the same thing.

    Bata's two CE labeling styles — the earlier Bata camera batch (cam*_malleshwaram, whitefield_cam*) labels people as Person (customer) vs Person with red t-shirt and black pant (employee).
